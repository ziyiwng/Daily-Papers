Analytic semigroup generated by the dispersal process of a sylvatic transmission model of Chagas disease
arXiv ID: 2604.26436v1
作者: Narimene Benarbia, Rabah Labbas, Tewfik Mahdjoub, Alexandre Thorel
更新时间: 2026-04-29 08:48
论文摘要: In this work, we develop a new biological transmission model for Chagas disease. This model, set in two juxtaposed habitats with skew Brownian motion conditions at the interface, is composed of two reaction--diffusion equations and takes into account the sylvatic transmission. We write it as an abstract perturbed Cauchy problem using operator theory. Then, we show that the main operator, which models the dispersal process, generates an analytic semigroup in an adequate Banach space.
核心总结: 问题：Chagas病在两种相邻生境中的传播建模；方法：构建包含偏微分方程和算子理论的扩散模型；结果：主算子在巴拿赫空间生成解析半群。
